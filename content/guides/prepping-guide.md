# Prepping Guide

This guide is being developed as a practical note on calm household preparedness. The emphasis is planning, redundancy, and proportional judgment rather than alarmism.

## Planned Topics

- water, food, and basic household supplies
- power outages, communication, and charging
- documents, medicine, and small emergency planning
- preparedness as steadiness rather than panic
