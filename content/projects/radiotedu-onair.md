## From programme to broadcast

OnAir brings scheduling, playout, and broadcast automation into a supervised workflow. It prepares music, spoken links, and scheduled output while keeping the operator in control of the programme.

## My role

I created and developed RadioTEDU OnAir. My work focuses on the system that prepares and plays the programme, with a clear hand-off to the separate streaming service that delivers it to listeners.
