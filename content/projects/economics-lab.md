## The question

How can economic decisions become easier to explore and discuss? Ekonomi Laboratuvarı provides an interactive setting for examining choices and their consequences.

## What you can explore

The simulation supports individual play and classroom use. Its web interface is available directly on this site; classroom services use a separate server. Individual play remains available when that server is offline.

## My contribution

I develop the simulation and maintain its integration with this site. The game has its own source repository, while the published interface lives alongside my writing and research.

## Try it

[Open Ekonomi Laboratuvarı](/econ/) to explore the simulation, or [browse the source code](https://github.com/akgularda/ekonomi-laboratuvari). The simulation interface is in Turkish.
