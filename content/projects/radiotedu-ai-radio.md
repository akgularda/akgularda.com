## A radio experiment

AI Radio explores how generated hosting and spoken links can fit the rhythm of a radio programme. Music, programme context, and narration form the material for the experiment.

## My role

I created and developed AI Radio within RadioTEDU. The project is a practical setting for exploring machine-assisted presentation and how it can support a listening experience.

## Project context

AI Radio is presented as an experiment in the [RadioTEDU project overview](https://radiotedu.com/technology/projects/ai-radio/).
