## A place to listen and participate

Social / Study World brings shared presence to RadioTEDU through avatars, live rooms, study sessions, chat, and reactions. Games and customization connect to account-based inventory and reward systems.

## My role

I created and developed this project. It connects listening with a shared campus environment and community activities, with account and inventory actions managed by the server.
