## Radio in the terminal

RadioTEDU Terminal brings listening into the command line on Windows, macOS, and Linux. It combines station selection, search, favourites, playback controls, a sleep timer, and focus sessions.

## My role

I created and developed the terminal application. Its keyboard-driven interface connects radio listening with a working environment people already use.

## Availability

The [terminal application page](https://radiotedu.com/tui) provides installation instructions, platform requirements, and links to the public source code.
