## Intelligence in a media workflow

RTAI is RadioTEDU's applied media intelligence practice. It connects work on AI Radio, local audio production, geographic news interfaces, and assistant-based discovery.

## My role

I created and developed RTAI as a framework for this work. The focus is on useful media tools and experiments that people can evaluate and incorporate into editorial and production workflows.

## Connected projects

- [AI Radio](/projects/radiotedu-ai-radio/): machine-assisted selection and presentation.
- [Situation Room](/projects/situation-room/): geographic news and source-linked briefings.
- [Agentic Web & MCP](/projects/agentic-web/): public media context for compatible assistants.

The [RTAI overview](https://radiotedu.com/technology/intelligence/) also documents audio-production work and areas of investigation.
