## Reading the world in context

Situation Room connects reports to a three-dimensional globe and an information timeline. Text and spoken summaries offer different ways to enter a briefing, with source links available for further reading.

## My role

I created and developed Situation Room. The project brings geographic navigation, a readable news interface, and source visibility into one experience.
