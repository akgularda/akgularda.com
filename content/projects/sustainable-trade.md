## The question

How do firms and institutions respond to the green transition? My research interests connect sustainable trade, energy policy, corporate ESG information, and the quality of sustainability reporting.

## My contribution

As an Undergraduate Research Assistant at TEDUsTRC since October 2024, I analyze corporate ESG information and synthesize literature and findings for research reports. I also supported sustainability research during a June–July 2026 internship.

My economics thesis examines EU exposure, the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), and sustainability reporting in the aluminum sector.

## Published work

I co-prepared the sTAM Monthly Sustainability Agenda, Issue 01, and the Q2 2026 Sustainability Agenda with Ezgi Eylem Erdoğan. These include interviews with Prof. Dr. Ayça Tekin-Koru and Prof. Dr. Erdem Başçı.
