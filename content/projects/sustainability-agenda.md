## Research in an editorial format

The sTAM Sustainability Agenda brings together developments in sustainable trade, climate policy, finance, corporate reporting, and research. Its issues combine editorial discussion, data, policy updates, and interviews.

## My role

I co-edit the bulletin with Ezgi Eylem Erdoğan at TED University’s Sustainable Trade Research Center. This work connects research and writing with an ongoing editorial publication.
