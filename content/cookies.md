# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** {{< current_date >}}

This Cookie Policy explains how **arda-akgul.com** uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your choices between visits. Similar technologies (such as local storage) may serve the same purpose; this policy covers them as well.

## Cookies We Set

### Strictly necessary / preference

| Name | Purpose | Duration | Set without consent? |
|------|---------|----------|----------------------|
| `ac_consent_v1` | Stores your consent choice (analytics on/off, advertising signals on/off) | 365 days | Yes — required to remember your decision |

### Analytics (Google Analytics 4 — only if you consent)

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Distinguishes unique users | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Persists session state | 2 years |

Analytics cookies are placed **only after** you select "Accept all" or save preferences with analytics enabled. Measurement ID: `G-RY2DML1TZX`.

### Advertising

The Site does not currently run advertising and does not set advertising cookies. Consent Mode signals for `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, and `ad_personalization` are still collected so that, if advertising is ever added, it will start in a consent-compliant state. Declining them has no effect on site functionality.

## Third-Party Cookies

We do not embed third-party widgets that set their own cookies (no social embeds, no video embeds, no ad networks). Third parties such as Google may still process technical data (for example your IP address) when their resources are loaded — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details on Google Analytics and Google Fonts.

## Managing Cookies

### Through our banner
Use **Cookie settings** in the site footer to reopen the consent banner and change your choice at any time.

### Through your browser
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking all cookies will prevent the Site from remembering your consent choice, so the banner will reappear on each visit.
