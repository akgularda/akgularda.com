# Geometry

Geometry matters because it trains the eye to think about form, proportion, proof, and structure with discipline. I want this section to explore geometry not only as a branch of mathematics, but as a way of seeing order in models, design, and reasoning.

## Planned Themes

- form, symmetry, and spatial reasoning
- proof, rigor, and mathematical discipline
- geometry in architecture and design
- geometric thinking in economics and computation
